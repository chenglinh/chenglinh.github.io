I’m a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Michigan, advised by Stella X. Yu.

Perception. I’m excited about embodied vision: understanding the world from partial observations. My interests span spatial and dynamic understanding and semantic localization, with the goal of supporting useful decisions and actions in the real world.

Robot learning. I’m interested in contact-rich whole-body control grounded in perception. I want to learn from large-scale priors, especially diverse human motion repertoires, and use training-free guidance to adapt that knowledge to new goals on humanoid robots.

NeurIPS 2026

Dense Flow from Adaptive Correspondence

Cheng-Lin Hsieh and Stella X. Yu

We show that learning what to match is as important as learning how to match. Our adaptive units follow coherent motion and preserve fine boundaries, making optical flow both precise and robust—even where a match is hidden or outside the image.

Under review

Test-Time Motion Steering for Perceptive Humanoid Fall Recovery

Osher Azulay*, Cheng-Lin Hsieh*, and Stella X. Yu

We turn getting back up into a behavior the robot can adapt and steer. A visual diffusion model lets us guide heading, timing, and route at test time, while a depth-aware controller carries out the recovery on a real Unitree G1.
