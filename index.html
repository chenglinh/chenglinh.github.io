I’m a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Michigan, advised by Stella X. Yu. I work on visual perception and robot learning: understanding motion in images and helping humanoid robots move in the real world.

My research spans dense visual correspondence and humanoid control, including hands-on work with the Unitree G1.

01

NeurIPS 2026

Dense Flow from Adaptive Correspondence

Cheng-Lin Hsieh and Stella X. Yu

We learn how to group pixels and match those groups together, rather than fixing the matching units in advance. This helps estimate motion across images and generalize to new scenes.

02

Under review

Test-Time Motion Steering for Perceptive Humanoid Fall Recovery

Osher Azulay*, Cheng-Lin Hsieh*, and Stella X. Yu

Getting back up is more useful when a robot can choose how and where it rises. We steer a learned motion plan at test time, helping a real Unitree G1 recover with a desired heading or route while responding to its surroundings.
