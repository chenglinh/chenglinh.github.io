My work connects humanoid control, visual-tactile learning, and dense correspondence, with hands-on real-robot deployment on a 29-DoF Unitree G1.

01

NeurIPS 2026

Dense Flow from Adaptive Correspondence

Cheng-Lin Hsieh and Stella X. Yu

Learns matching units through differentiable pixel-to-token assignment, jointly training grouping and matching for dense visual correspondence.

Lowest reported zero-shot endpoint errors on Sintel Final and KITTI among the published methods compared in the study.

02

Under review

Test-Time Motion Steering for Perceptive Humanoid Fall Recovery

Osher Azulay*, Cheng-Lin Hsieh*, and Stella X. Yu

A visual diffusion planner predicts future body states. Task costs steer the motion at test time toward a desired heading, rise profile, or route, and a depth-aware decoder executes it.

20/20flat-ground recoveries
9/10stair recoveries
11/11steered trials

Evaluation on a 29-DoF Unitree G1.

In preparation

Learning from Human Visual-Tactile Experience for Precise Contact-Aware Humanoid Object Operation

Cheng-Lin Hsieh*, Osher Azulay*, and Stella X. Yu

Learning policies that combine human visual-tactile experience with stable locomotion and precise contact. Current work includes deployment on the Unitree G1 for wheeled-object operation.
